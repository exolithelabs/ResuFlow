    Built in · read-only

    ResuFlow speaks MCP over stdio: your agent starts it as a local subprocess. There's no server, port or token, and nothing leaves this machine except what your agent sends to its own provider.

    
              
    Once connected, your agent can read the default template and your instructions, use the built-in skills, and read and edit your resumes. Only you can change instructions. To give the agent its own copy of the skills, open any skill and click Install.
